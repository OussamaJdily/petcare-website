A calm stay, medically supervised

Boarding at PetCare+ means your pet is looked after by people who understand animals, with veterinarians on site if anything changes.

Pets enjoy cosy private suites, regular walks and play, familiar food routines and plenty of affection. Medication and special diets are handled carefully and logged.

Daily updates

We send a photo and a short note every day, so you can relax and enjoy your time away, knowing your pet is happy and well.
